## Research standard

Each future vendor or technology entry should ideally store:
- canonical URL
- publisher / vendor
- page title
- accessed date
- relevant category
- key claims
- whether claim is vendor-supplied or independent
- last verified date

The site should distinguish:
1. vendor claims
2. independent facts
3. AIIngestion.com analysis / inference
4. benchmark results
